# Plan a shoot

1. `list_formats` and pick the closest of Director's formats with the person (launch, walk and talk, couch interview, product walkthrough, problem to solution, customer story, founder story).
2. `shoot_questions`: ask only what you can't infer from the conversation or `memory_get`, then `plan_shoot` with the answers.
3. Show the plan: beats, shots, lines and length. Change shots with `update_shot`.
4. When they film, `next_shot` with the shoot sets up each shot in the app (its lines on the prompter, its cameras); `shot_done` records the take and whether to keep it. Product screen shots can be recorded by you, as in the record-a-demo skill.
5. `shoot_status` shows what's left; `assemble_shoot` makes the edit when they're done. Then edit and export as usual.
